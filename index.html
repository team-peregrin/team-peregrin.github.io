The Challenge

Hospitals are stressful. Children carry that stress.

Pediatric procedural anxiety is well-documented but chronically under-addressed. Kids who feel unprepared face longer recovery times and lasting fear of medical care. Child life specialists told us directly: the tools are not good enough.

♥

Fear amplifies pain

When children do not understand what is happening to their bodies, the unknown amplifies physical sensation. Preparation measurably reduces perceived pain and distress.

●

Families are left guessing

Parents often receive clinical language that does not translate to a child. They need tools to co-regulate alongside their children — not separately from them.
